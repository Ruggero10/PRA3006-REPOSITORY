Type 1-Diabetes and Autoimmune diseases

Our Research

One of the most known autoimmune diseases is type 1 diabetes mellitus, but is there also a correlation with the others autoimmune diseases? Our goal is to provide a list of autoimmune diseases that have a connection with type 1 diabetes mellitus through shared genes in order to facilitate a better understanding of how these diseases can be connected and to facilitate further therapeutic research in this field.
